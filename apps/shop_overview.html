Definitions

  • Net revenue: quantity × (unit price − discount), for completed and shipped orders only.
  • Orders: every order in the period, any status. AOV: net revenue ÷ completed and shipped orders.
  • Buyers: distinct customers with an order. Gross margin: (net revenue − cost of goods) ÷ net revenue.
  • Dates are order dates in UTC. Numbers are rounded for display only.
